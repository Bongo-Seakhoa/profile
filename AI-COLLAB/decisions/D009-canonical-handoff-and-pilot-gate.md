# D009 - Canonical handoff and pilot reconstruction gate

**Date:** 2026-07-31
**Owner:** Bongo Seakhoa
**Implementer:** Codex
**Status:** Adopted

## Decision

Use the verified Desert Nomad Complete Handoff v3.0 as the canonical roster,
measurement, garment-layer, material and written-detail source for immersive
character production.

The fifteen roster identities are now fixed. The earlier dependency stating
that no canonical roster existed is closed.

## Boundary

The handoff contains reference documentation rather than finished production
meshes. Its simplified A-pose drawings cannot be treated as final anatomy or
surface orthographics.

`DN-M-AFR-01` is the first pilot because its full-coverage requirement,
asymmetric mantle, jewellery, pouches, scarf, tassel, footwear and power
silhouette exercise the strictest cross-system gates.

## Pilot exit criteria

The pilot must have:

- approved reconstructed modelling boards;
- a dimensionally checked Blender source scene;
- modular garments and accessory collision envelopes;
- the shared rig and attachment sockets;
- PBR material calibration and recolour masks;
- at least three measured runtime LODs;
- the compact animation set and representative traversal clips;
- valid full-body camera telemetry through every visible phase;
- a browser export and visual/performance evidence; and
- no default weapon, eye tracking, blinking or lip sync.

Only after this pilot passes may the same pipeline scale to the other fourteen
characters.

## Superseding camera authority

All older "rear", "chase" or camera-exit wording in the handoff is interpreted
through D004 and the owner's later corrections. The avatar remains fully
contained in a distant third-person composition. Power phases may use safe
middle or upper pockets and Sand Teleportation may use its typed, bounded
visibility suppression. No OTS path is allowed.
