# D010 - Owner rights and pilot approval

**Date:** 2026-07-31
**Owner:** Bongo Seakhoa
**Implementer:** Codex
**Status:** Adopted

## Owner confirmation

Bongo Seakhoa confirms that he owns the supplied Desert Nomad images and has
the authority to approve their use.

The owner authorizes Codex and project contributors to create derivatives from
the supplied images and to publish and redistribute those derivatives as part
of this portfolio project.

This confirmation resolves `DN-CHAR-RIGHTS-001` for the authorized portfolio
scope.

## Pilot approval

The owner approves the `DN-M-AFR-01` v2 silhouette for production.

This approval establishes the v2 silhouette as the pilot's accepted shape and
proportion direction. It does not waive the dimensional, garment, rig,
material, animation, full-body camera, browser, accessibility, performance or
quality gates recorded in D009 and the immersive production evaluation matrix.

## Privacy, attribution and provenance boundary

- Raw reference masters remain private and must not be published or copied
  into the public deployment.
- Only approved derivatives and production-ready runtime assets may enter the
  public portfolio.
- Asset manifests must preserve source hashes, derivative lineage, version and
  approval status.
- Existing attribution and provenance controls remain mandatory for every
  public derivative.
- This decision grants no permission to expose unrelated private source files
  or third-party material outside the confirmed owner-controlled pack.

## Consequence

Character production may proceed from the approved `DN-M-AFR-01` v2
silhouette, subject to the remaining D009 pilot acceptance gates. Scaling to
the other fourteen characters still requires a fully validated pilot.
