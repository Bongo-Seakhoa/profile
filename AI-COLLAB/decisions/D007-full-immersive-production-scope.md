## Owner direction

Static View is the professional foundation and complete fallback. It is not the
final limit of the Profile Upgrade project. The production scope also includes:

- the complete approved canonical character roster, selection presentation,
  modelling, rigging, materials, accessories and LODs;
- shared male, female and nonbinary locomotion profiles plus character-specific
  adjustments required by canon or silhouette;
- gesture, idle, interaction, traversal and power animation, including Solar
  Propulsion, Sand Teleportation, surfing, launches, landings, look-back and
  edge lean;
- every approved Anzania location set, with lighting, atmosphere, particles,
  environmental movement, background enhancement and transitions;
- the opt-in immersive browser runtime, asset streaming, quality tiers,
  performance budgets, recovery and professional Static View fallback; and
- Blender and browser evaluation across every character, set, animation, power,
  viewport ratio, content layout, input mode and quality tier.

## Sequencing

- Release 1 completes the Static View and browser-native professional documents.
- M7 validates the production contracts with a vertical slice before the asset
  pipeline scales.
- M8 to M12 build, integrate, evaluate and release the complete immersive scope.
- A failed vertical slice returns to implementation. It cannot be used to remove
  the immersive milestones.

## Camera and animation boundary

- The camera is distant, full-body and third-person. No OTS path is permitted.
- Ordinary animation lives primarily in the lower-third home stage.
- Authored power phases may temporarily use safe middle or upper screen pockets
  and must return deterministically.
- Every intentionally visible frame contains the complete animated character
  bounds, accessories and power-relevant silhouette.
- Only named, duration-limited authored phases such as Sand Teleportation may
  suppress visibility.

## Dependency boundary

The missing approved canonical character reference pack blocks truthful
character-roster production. It does not make the immersive scope optional.
Work that does not require inventing canonical facts may continue while that
pack is pending.

## Acceptance

The project is not complete until M8 to M12 pass or a genuinely external
dependency is recorded with the exact missing input, owner and recovery path.
